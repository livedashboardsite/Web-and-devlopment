Ajeenkya DY Patil College

Vedam School of Technology

Ajeenkya DY Patil College, home to the Vedam School of Technology, is a premier institute dedicated to shaping future-ready engineers and technologists. The college is known for its modern infrastructure, industry-driven curriculum, and a strong focus on emerging technologies like Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. I am currently pursuing my B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering with a specialization in Artificial Intelligence (CSE AI) here. The campus provides an inspiring environment with well-equipped labs, experienced faculty, and numerous opportunities for research, hackathons, and industry collaborations that help students turn their ideas into reality.

Top 5 College Facilities

  1. Central Library

    A multi-story library with over 2 lakh books, digital resources, and dedicated reading halls.

  2. Sports Complex

    Indoor and outdoor stadiums with facilities for cricket, football, basketball, badminton, and a swimming pool.

  3. Research & Innovation Park

    Dedicated labs and incubation centers for students to work on cutting-edge research projects and startups.

  4. Student Activity Center

    Hosts cultural fests, technical events, and houses various societies and club offices.

  5. Modern Cafeteria & Food Courts

    Multiple eating joints offering diverse cuisines with comfortable seating and a lively atmosphere.
